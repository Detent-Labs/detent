## Why

`PONYTAIL-AUDIT.md`'s 2026-08-16 scan leaves findings with no open change.
Two of them are the cheapest left that touch no file the five open
`ponytail-*` changes reach. Finding 32 removes the `immer` dependency, which
one call site earns. Finding 39 is a grouped list of small pairs; four of its
entries sit in `packages/web`.

The audit files finding 39 under "small enough to ride along with whatever
touches their files next". Nothing is touching those files. Of the five open
changes, none reaches `draft/store.tsx`, either area's `errors.ts`,
`reporting/api/client.ts` or `app/api/client.ts`. So these wait for a carrier
that is not coming.

A grep sweep re-measured every entry before this proposal. Two hold as
written. Three cost more than they save. This change lands the first two and
writes the three corrections back into the audit.

## What Changes

- Drop `immer` from `packages/web/package.json` (finding 32). `produce` has
  one call site, the `mutate` case of the draft reducer. It becomes
  `structuredClone(state.draft)`, the recipe run against that copy, and the
  copy returned.
- Replace the `import type { Draft as Immer } from "immer"` alias in
  `draft/store.tsx` and `draft/draft-array-crud.ts` with the local `Draft`
  type. `Draft` is `DraftOf<AuthoredProcessBody>`, which maps every property
  to optional and declares no `readonly`. Immer's `Draft<T>` strips
  `readonly`, so against this type the two are the same type.
- Record the trade in a `ponytail:` comment on the reducer. `produce` shares
  the subtrees a recipe leaves alone; `structuredClone` copies the whole body.
  The comment names that ceiling and the way back.
- Drop the `export` keyword from `reducer`, `ReducerState` and `Action` in
  `draft/store.tsx` (finding 32). Their comment justifies the export with
  `draft-store-reducer.test.ts`. That file does not exist, and nothing outside
  `store.tsx` reads any of the three.
- Delete `describeError`'s unused `_status` parameter in
  `areas/admin/errors.ts` and `areas/studio/errors.ts` (finding 39). Nine
  call sites pass it, three in `src` and six in
  `packages/web/test/errors.test.ts`. Each comment justifying the parameter
  goes with it; design.md decision 5 says why that reason is circular.
- Delete the `export { AppClientError as ReportingClientError }` alias in
  `areas/reporting/api/client.ts` and point its three references at
  `AppClientError` (finding 39).
- Merge `listMyTasks` and `listStartedByMe` in `areas/app/api/client.ts` into
  one `listInstances(scope, token, opts)` (finding 39). The two agree except
  for the `scope` value. The four call sites in `TasksScreen.tsx` and
  `StartedScreen.tsx` pass the scope they already mean. The comment stating
  why `scope=started` sends no `startedBy` of its own stays with that case.
- Correct `PONYTAIL-AUDIT.md`. Record findings 32 and the landed half of 39
  as resolved. Move the three declined entries under "Checked, not flagged",
  each with the measurement that disqualifies it. `design.md` carries the
  evidence.

Three entries of finding 39 do not land:

- Inline `shell/accountName.ts` into `Chrome.tsx`. The file has its own test,
  `packages/web/test/chrome-accountName.test.ts`, covering three cases.
  Inlining deletes that coverage with nowhere for it to move. This is the
  reason `ponytail-cleanup-fetch-hooks-and-imports` declined finding 37.
- Derive `onGoToArea` and `onGoToProfile` from the `go` prop. The four area
  roots build `/${a}`. `shell/App.tsx` builds `areaHref(a, "/")` at its two
  call sites. Two derivations, so no single prop replaces both.
- Merge `listComments` and `listAttachments`. Two things differ, the path
  segment and the return type. One returns a comment page and the other an
  attachment page. A type parameter over two call sites adds code rather than
  removing it.
